Q&A Format

What is the Pythagorean Theorem?
It is a formula used in right-angled triangles. It states that:
a2 + b2 = c2
What is the value of Pi (π)?
The value of π (Pi) is approximately 3.14159, and it's used in circles.

Explanation in Simple Points

Pythagorean Theorem
  • Used only in right-angled triangles.
  • The two shorter sides are 'a' and 'b'.
  • The longest side (opposite the right angle) is 'c'.
  • Formula: a2 + b2 = c2
  • Helps to find the length of a missing side.
Pi (π)
  • π is used in formulas involving circles.
  • π = 3.14159 (commonly rounded to 3.14).
  • Circumference = 2πr
  • Area of Circle = πr2
  • π is an irrational number (goes on forever).
